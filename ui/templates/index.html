Dualpanto Testing Framework

Start by testing the firmware upload

Upload firmware test

Upload a hello world firmware to the Panto to check if a connection can be established.

1. open config.py and enter your COM port to connect dualpanto.
2. All tests upload a firmware for each unit-test. You probably need to push button back of dualpanto when you upload a firmware (it depends on OS).

Encoder Motor Automatic Test

Test if the handles move freely and the encoders work

1. Connect charged battery (and flip battery switch in direction of the middle of the panto)
2. Clear the space the handles can reach and pay attention that the battery is not touching the handles
3. Bring the handles in the default positon
4. Start test and press the upload button
